# Safe Haven waiver app: pilot guide

For the staff at **Safe Haven for Animals** trying the waiver app on the front-desk tablet
(LG G Pad X 8.0). Setup takes about 20 minutes, needs no computer, and you only do it once.

Questions or problems: contact Salman (see "If something goes wrong" at the end).

## What you get

One file, **`SafeHaven-waiver-0.1.0.apk`**, sent as a download link. It's the app itself.

## 1. Before you install

1. Charge the tablet and connect it to the shelter's Wi-Fi.
2. Check the time: **Settings → Date & time**. Turn on **Automatic date & time**, and make sure
   the time zone is **Arizona** (Mountain Standard Time, *not* "Mountain Time" with daylight
   saving). Every waiver is dated with the tablet's clock.

## 2. Install the app

1. On the tablet, open the download link and download the file.
2. Open the downloaded file (pull down the notification, or open the **Downloads** app and tap
   it).
3. If the tablet says **"Install blocked"**: tap **Settings**, turn on **Unknown sources**, tap
   **OK**, then go back and open the file again. (Android asks this once for any app that doesn't
   come from the Play Store.)
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**. You'll see the Safe Haven welcome screen.
5. Optional: turn **Unknown sources** back off (Settings → Security, or Lock screen & security).
   You'll need it on again to install an update.

## 3. Keyboard settings

Visitors type their personal details, so stop the keyboard from remembering them:
**Settings → Language & input →** the keyboard in use (for example LG Keyboard or Google
Keyboard):

- turn **off** word suggestions / prediction, and anything like "learn words" or "personalized
  suggestions";
- turn **off** voice typing / voice input;
- turn **off** any clipboard feature (LG calls it "Clip Tray").

## 4. Keep visitors in the app (screen pinning)

The pilot version doesn't lock the tablet by itself. Android's own **screen pinning** keeps the
waiver app on screen so visitors can't wander into other apps.

**Turn it on (once):**

1. **Settings → Security** (on some LG tablets: **Lock screen & security**) **→ Screen pinning**
   (or "Pin windows") → **On**.
2. Recommended: in the same screen, turn on **Ask for PIN before unpinning** (or "Lock device
   when unpinning"). This needs a screen lock PIN on the tablet; choose one staff will know.
   Without it, anyone who knows the trick in step "Unpin" below can leave the app.

**Pin the app (each time the tablet is restarted):**

1. Open the Safe Haven app.
2. Tap the **Recents** button (the square at the bottom of the screen). If other apps are listed
   too, close them with their **X** so Safe Haven is the only card.
3. On the Safe Haven card, tap the round **pin** button (bottom right of the card).
4. Android says **"Screen is pinned"**. Tap **GOT IT**.

While pinned, Home, Recents and the notification shade do nothing, even after the screen turns
off and on.

**Unpin** (staff only): touch and hold **Back** and **Recents** (Android calls it "Overview")
at the same time. If you turned on the PIN option, enter the tablet's screen lock PIN.

## 5. Create the staff PIN (before any visitor uses the tablet)

1. On the welcome screen, **press and hold the logo** for about a second.
2. Choose a **staff PIN** of 4 to 8 digits, then enter it again.
3. **Write the PIN down** and keep it somewhere safe at the shelter.

There is no default PIN: until one is created, the first person to press and hold the logo
creates it. That's why this step comes first.

**If the PIN is forgotten,** the waivers on the tablet can't be opened from the app until
Salman resets it, which needs the tablet connected to a computer. Keep the PIN written down.

## 6. Every day

**Visitors** tap **Start**, fill in their details, read the waiver, type their initials next to
each paragraph and sign with a finger. A **parent or guardian** fills in and signs for anyone
under 18: the form switches over by itself once the date of birth is entered. **Submit** saves
the waiver and shows "Thank you".

- To sign, draw inside the white box. To scroll the page, drag *outside* the box. **Clear**
  starts the signature again.
- A form left untouched for 90 seconds asks "Are you still there?", then clears itself, so the
  next visitor never sees someone else's details.

**Staff area:** press and hold the logo, then enter the staff PIN.

- **Records:** every waiver, newest first. Search by name, phone, email or record number. Tap
  one to see the PDF, **Share** it (for example by email; unpin the screen first), or
  **Delete** it.
- **Backup:** see below.
- **Lock** returns to the welcome screen. The staff area also locks itself after 2 minutes
  without a touch.

## 7. Backups (important)

Until a waiver is backed up, **it exists only on this tablet.** If the tablet is lost, broken or
reset, those waivers are gone.

**Once:** staff area → **Backup** → **Choose folder…**. In the window that opens, tap the menu
(☰) and choose where the copies go: **Google Drive** (if it's listed) or an **SD card / USB
stick**. Open or create a folder (for example "Waivers") and tap **Select**.

**At the end of each day:** staff area → **Backup** → **Back up now**. The screen shows how many
waivers are waiting and when the last backup ran.

Backups work while the screen is pinned. **Sharing a waiver doesn't**: Android won't open
email or other apps over a pinned screen, and the app says so. Unpin first, share, then pin
again.

Each backup file is named by date and record number, for example `2026-11-18_0001.pdf`.

## 8. Never do these

- **Never uninstall the app**, and never use **Clear data** / **Clear storage** for it in
  Settings → Apps. Either one **deletes every waiver on the tablet**, including any not backed up.
- **Never factory reset the tablet** without a backup.
- Don't install a copy of the app from anywhere except Salman's link.

## 9. Updates

Salman will send a new file. Open it the same way as in step 2 and tap **Install** (or
**Update**). The waivers and the staff PIN are kept. **Don't uninstall the old version first.**

## 10. If something goes wrong

Tell Salman:

- what you were doing, and what happened;
- the date and time;
- the record number, if it's about a saved waiver;
- a photo of the screen taken with a phone, if you can. (The tablet blocks screenshots of the
  app on purpose: the screens show personal information.)

## What the pilot version doesn't do yet

- **No full kiosk lock.** Screen pinning keeps casual visitors in the app, but it isn't as strict
  as a locked kiosk. A locked kiosk is possible later; it needs a video call with someone at the
  shelter and a factory reset of the tablet (after a backup).
- **The waivers on the tablet aren't encrypted.** The staff PIN protects them in the app; keep
  the tablet at the desk.
- **No automatic backups.** Someone has to tap **Back up now**.
